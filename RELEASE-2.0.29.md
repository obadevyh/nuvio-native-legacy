Saved thumbnails are kept safe, and pausing no longer makes the timing check fail.

- Pausing the film no longer counts against the timing check that matches a smaller copy to the film. Before, a long pause could make a good copy run out of time and fall back to the slower route.
- If that check rejects a copy or can't reach a result, thumbnails saved from an earlier play are kept, along with their file. Before, a whole saved set could be deleted. This happened once on Fight Club, when 835 thumbnails were lost.

Both fixes were found and tested on a Meta Quest 3, which shares this code.

Not yet confirmed: these fixes have not been run on a TV. The TV build compiles with them.
