Faster seek thumbnails for 4K films: Nuvio now makes them from a smaller copy of the same film in your source list (720p or 1080p), checked against what is on screen before it is kept.

On the tested Shield, Dune: Part Two in 4K got its full thumbnail bar in 67 seconds from TorBox (was about 99) and in 73 seconds from a PenguPlay source (was about 3 minutes), downloading about 70-105 MB instead of about 700 MB, with no rebuffering. Thumbnails start within a few seconds; if the copy does not match the film, it is discarded and the 4K file itself is used as before. 1080p and lower titles are unchanged (a 22-minute episode took 15 seconds).

Also: each file's keyframe positions are remembered on the device so a second play skips the index read, several start-up stalls on PenguPlay sources are fixed, and a tip in Settings explains that 4K previews are fastest when your add-ons also list 720p or 1080p WEB-DL sources.

These are observed results on one device and connection, not a guarantee for every title or box.
