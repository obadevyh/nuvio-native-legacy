## Nuvio Legacy 2.0.10 (sharing build)

Brings this build up to date with the latest Nuvio Legacy. OpenSeek, contributor mode and in-app updates work as before.

New from Nuvio Legacy:
* Live TV: add your own M3U playlists and XMLTV guides, a faster guide search, and channel previews that keep playing.
* Upscaling: choose the upscaling method, and better HDR on supported TVs.
* Arabic interface language.
* A Library row on Home with your saved titles.
* A notice when sources for a movie become available.
* Anime: absolute episode numbers kept in Continue Watching.
* Text size setting, review pages with filters, episode ratings with the air date.
* Playback speed changes now work with every audio format.
* A Clear cache action, and smoother playback on TVs with little free memory.

Your account, settings, Simkl login and OpenSeek pairing stay as they are.
